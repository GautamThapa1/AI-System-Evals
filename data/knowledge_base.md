# Acme Cloud Help Center
Acme Cloud offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Enterprise. The Free plan
includes 5 GB of storage and 1 project. The Pro plan costs $12 per user per
month and includes 500 GB of storage and unlimited projects. The Enterprise
plan has custom pricing and includes single sign-on (SSO).
The refund window for Pro subscriptions is 30 days from purchase. Annual
billing is 20% cheaper than monthly billing. Refunds are issued to the
original payment method.
Support is available by email at support@acmecloud.example. Email support
responds within 24 hours on weekdays. Enterprise customers also get 24/7
phone support with a 1 hour response time for critical issues.
To reset your password, click Forgot password on the login page. The password
reset link is valid for 60 minutes.
Two-factor authentication works with authenticator apps. SMS codes are not
supported. Enterprise admins are required to enable two-factor
authentication.
Deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days and are then permanently deleted.
File version history is kept for 90 days on Pro and Enterprise plans, and 7
days on the Free plan.
The API rate limit is 100 requests per minute on the Free plan and 1,000
requests per minute on the Pro plan. Requests over the limit receive an HTTP
429 error.
Acme Cloud has data centers in Frankfurt, Virginia, and Singapore. You choose
a region when you create a project, and it cannot be changed later.
The Enterprise plan includes a 99.9% uptime SLA. The Free and Pro plans do
not include an SLA.
